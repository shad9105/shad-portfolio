About

Veterinary science wasn’t my plan. Circumstances pointed me here, and I decided to commit fully instead of looking back.

Since then I’ve worked with biology as a script evaluator and Q&A teacher for HSC and admission students, and built small web tools for my classmates at BAU.

Away from study I take photographs and I’m learning Spanish. Both keep me paying attention to the world around me.
